# Setup & Tuning Option Reference

This page documents the normal user-facing controls in RealSweatSystem 2.0.0. Controls marked **Base** appear when the Sweat Base Module is installed; controls marked **Effects** require the Sweat Effects Module.

## Open the Tuning component

After Setup, expand the avatar in the Hierarchy and select the generated **RealSweat** object directly under the avatar root. Its Inspector contains **RealSweatSystem Tuning**.

If the object is missing because the first Setup did not finish, resolve the first Console error and press **[Install / Update]** again. If an installed `RealSweat` object was deleted manually, do not rerun Setup without checking the Body first; follow [manual-deletion recovery](../troubleshooting.mdx#manual-delete-recovery).

## When changes take effect

| Change | When it takes effect |
|---|---|
| **Sweat Quality** | Material quality changes immediately. Press **Apply to FX** to write every other change into the generated FX assets. |
| **Sweat Style** (Tuning: **Basic Settings > Sweat Style**) | Applies to every sweat material of the avatar at once. |
| **Droplet Size** | The Droplet Depth and flat surface materials update immediately. Press **Apply to FX** to also refresh falling-drop and strong-movement-fling scale. |
| **3D Detail Precision** | Updates the installed Droplet Depth material immediately. |
| Automatic-response, accumulation-curve, retention, effect, and advanced FX values | Press **Apply to FX**. |
| Body material Shadow Strength | Press **Apply to FX**, or press **[Install / Update]** again, to copy it. |
| Custom Steam Point values | Press **Apply to FX** or **[Install / Update]**. |
| Sweat targets, other meshes, installed effects, 2D/3D particles, Drop Splash, mirror options, first-person Breath, intensity radial, render order, and installed Droplet Depth | Press **[Install / Update]**. |

The accumulation graphs preview pending values immediately, but they do not modify generated FX until **Apply to FX** is pressed. If **Apply to FX** reports an installation configuration change, press **[Install / Update]** first; adding or removing installed effects cannot be completed with Apply to FX alone.

## Setup window option reference

Open `CookiTools → RealSweatSystem → Setup／Uninstall`. The tabs are **Install** / **Troubleshooting** / **Uninstall**. The first screen shows only what you need; the detailed options are folded under **Advanced Settings**.

### First screen

| Control | Module | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Avatar** | All | None | Select your avatar in the Hierarchy or drag it into this field. |
| **Status** | All | — | Shows whether the avatar is installed. An avatar installed with an earlier version shows the yellow box "Installed with an older version." |
| **Sweat on** | All | Auto-detect | The body mesh is found automatically and shown on this line. |
| **Will install** | All | — | Shows what Setup will install. |
| **Sweat Style** | Base | **Anime (default)** | Click one of two pictures: **Anime (default)** or **Realistic**. When you open the window again, the avatar's current style is selected. |
| **Look** | Base / Effects | **Flat** | Click one of two pictures. **Depth (High Load)** turns on Droplet Depth, 3D Particles, and Drop Splash together and shows **On from the Start** under it. **Flat** turns Droplet Depth and 3D Particles off. |
| **Load** | All | **Low Load (Recommended)** | Buttons: **Low Load (Recommended)** or **Standard**. **Lightweight** is in **Advanced Settings > Quality & Load**. |
| **Auto Mode Sensitivity** | All | **Balanced (recommended)** | **Balanced (recommended)** or **Sensitive**. |
| **[Install / Update]** | All | — | Installs or updates the avatar. The result appears in the box right under the button, and the longer steps show a progress bar. |

### Advanced Settings

| Control | Module | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Sweat Target** | Base | Best detected skin Element | If the body has several materials, check only the skin materials here. Other meshes such as a separate face can be added as **experimental** targets with independent Rest data, masks, and draw cost. |
| **Effects to Install** | Effects | On | Chooses falling drops, floor stains, breath, and body steam before installation. Floor stains require falling drops. |
| **Quality & Load > Sweat Quality** | All | Low Load (Recommended) | The dropdown with all three tiers: **Standard**, **Low Load (Recommended)**, **Lightweight**. |
| **Quality & Load > 3D Particles (High Load)** | Effects | Off | Changes only rendering to mesh particles; gravity, collision, floor stains, and effect toggles remain. Switching between 2D and 3D requires **[Install / Update]**. |
| **Quality & Load > Drop Splash** | Effects | Off with **Flat** | A short splash of small round droplets, in 2D and 3D. Turns on when you choose **Depth (High Load)**, and can also be turned on or off by itself. Adds 2 particle systems and 2 material slots. |
| **Quality & Load > Droplet Depth** | Base | Off | **Droplet Depth (Tessellation, High Load)**. Off installs only `RealSweatSystem No Tessellation`. On installs the Tess/No Tess material pair and adds **Droplet Depth (Tessellation / High Load)** under **Reduce Load**. **On from the Start** is under this toggle. |
| **Compatibility > [Ask Again]** | Base | — | After **Keep As Is**, press it and then **[Install / Update]** to decide the render-queue question again. |

### Other Setup options

| Control | Module | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Automatic render order** | Base | On | Draws the primary Body Sweat at render queue `2350`. Additional targets use `max(body sweat render queue (usually 2350), selected material render queue + 1)`. |
| **Manual render queue** | Base | — | Use it only when another shader requires a specific order. With a manual queue, Setup neither asks about nor changes body materials. |
| **Wet Skin Gloss as Sweat Increases** | Base | On for new installations | Enables the wet-skin gloss response. Updates follow the installed material state. |
| **High-Quality Sweat in Mirrors (High Load)** | Base | Off | While it is off, mirrors leave out the small pore droplets and Droplet Depth does not rise there. When it is on, mirrors draw the same as the normal view, at a higher mirror GPU load. |
| **Write Defaults Compatibility** | All | Auto Detect (Recommended) | Uses all-WD-ON RealSweat states only when every external FX state uses WD ON; otherwise uses the Driver ON / Output OFF compatible split. |
| **Body Steam & Breath Intensity radial (+8 bits)** | Effects | Off | Adds a saved synchronized Float. 0% = 0.1× opacity, 50% = current appearance at 1.0×, 100% = 1.5×. This changes opacity only and does not reduce particle or performance cost. |
| **Steam locations** | Effects | Chest, shoulders, armpits, thighs on; crotch and feet off | Chooses which body-steam emitters Setup creates. |

Follow Body Shadow Strength, droplet-depth precision, Body Steam in mirrors, Show My Breath in First Person, and Custom Steam Points are set after install in Tuning on the `RealSweat` object.

Before writing assets, Setup checks all planned parameters against Avatar Expression Parameters and other Modular Avatar declarations, including prefixes and remaps. A name collision stops Setup. A projected total above 256 bits produces a warning before continuing.

### Write Defaults Compatibility

- **Auto Detect (Recommended):** Uses WD ON for every RealSweat state only when every external FX state uses WD ON. WD OFF, mixed, or unknown setups use the WD OFF-compatible structure.
- **WD ON Compatibility:** Forces every RealSweat state to WD ON. Use it only when an avatar or MMD environment is known to require an all-WD-ON FX controller.
- **WD OFF Compatibility:** Keeps the v1.0.1 Driver ON / Output OFF split for WD OFF or mixed avatars.

### Sweat Quality

**Sweat Quality** (Overall Sweat Amount in 1.1.x; the same name in Tuning) picks the sweat's quality tier and load.

| Choice | What it draws |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Everything. The heaviest tier. |
| **Low Load (Recommended)** — the default for new installs | Leaves out only the small pore droplets. At conversation distance and at 1.5-3 m it is hard to tell from Standard. |
| **Lightweight** | Leaves out a little more than Low Load. Anime: the residual beads on trails and the thin film lines (the film's sheen stays); the droplets, the long streaks, and the trail sheen are the same as in Low Load. Realistic: the residual beads, the ridge highlight on pale skin, the long running streaks, the reflection probe, and the tiny droplets, so its droplets sit a little differently. Lightweight costs about the same GPU time as Low Load. |

- The amount of skin sweat is the same for all three. The material sweat amount is 0.30 at 30% on the Sweat % radial and 1.00 at 100%. In 1.1.x Lightweight and Low Load (then called "Low amount") used 0.20 and 0.65 there, so avatars that used them now look sweatier.
- Falling drops are still the most at Standard and fewer at Low Load and Lightweight. The 2D body steam is the same in all three; the 3D particle body steam keeps v1.1.3's amount, 0.65× Standard's at Low Load and Lightweight.

### Sweat Style

- **Anime (default)** has droplets with crisp highlights that read well from afar. **Realistic** has many small droplets, drawn with small reflections of the light and the surroundings instead of white highlights, with refraction and a dark rim. Clusters of tiny droplets sit between them, and from afar it reads as a wet gloss rather than dots. Only the big droplets run, and their trails are thin and faint.
- The style is one per avatar: the body and the other meshes (their Droplet Depth pairs included) all use the same style. After installing, you can change it in Tuning (**Basic Settings > Sweat Style**); the material inspector only shows the current style.
- New installs and avatars updated from 1.1.x start with Anime.
- The quality tiers apply to both styles. Realistic's Lightweight also leaves out the tiny droplets.
- Cost: Realistic has slightly fewer shader instructions than Anime (3445 against 3506 at Standard). Its GPU time is about the same, up to about 14% more when the whole torso fills the screen up close. With Droplet Depth it costs about 1.07× Anime's Droplet Depth at Standard and about 0.95× at Low Load. An avatar uses only the shader of its own style, so other players' first load is about the same whichever style you pick.
- Good to know: Realistic shows less than Anime at talking distance, in dark worlds, and in worlds without a reflection probe (at least 0.55× as visible). In folds such as above the navel a small droplet can look split in two, and droplets do not really merge.

### Droplet Depth choices {/* #droplet-depth-choices */}

When Droplet Depth is on, droplets and running droplets really rise from the skin up close and look round from the side, in both styles. Trails, small pore droplets, and the residual beads on trails stay flat.

| 3D Detail Precision | Droplets rise within about | Draws the same as without Droplet Depth from about |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Simplified** (default) | 0.35 m | 0.7 m |
| **Balanced** | 0.6 m | 1.5 m |
| **Precise** | 0.9 m | 2.2 m |

Installing Droplet Depth does not make both variants render together on one target. The menu switches every Droplet Depth sweat slot between Tess and No Tess. Turning **Droplet Depth (Tessellation / High Load)** off uses the No Tess shader, so hull/domain tessellation work is not performed. Droplets do not rise in mirrors while **High-Quality Sweat in Mirrors** is off, or under **Remote Low Load**.

For multiple configured targets, every target renders only its selected Tess or No Tess variant, but separate Body Elements and Renderers still add separate sweat draws.

### Experimental target safety

`RealSweatSystem Tuning` shows the generated material/slot status of installed additional targets.

- Use **Manage Targets...** to open Setup with the current target list loaded.
- Use **Open Setup to Repair...** when a target is marked **Repair Required**.
- To remove a target, remove it with `-` in the list and press **[Install / Update]**.
- Do not disable a target by deleting, clearing, moving, replacing, or merging its generated material or Renderer slot. Damage is not silently skipped; it blocks the avatar build until the target is repaired or removed.

## Tuning option reference

### Automatic Accumulation Response

| Control | Default | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Sensitivity Preset** | Balanced | **Balanced** follows movement strength. **Sensitive** reaches full response sooner and reacts more to small motion. Selecting a preset resets its response threshold, saturation point, rise/decay reference, and smoothing values. |
| **Build Speed Multiplier** | `1.0` | Multiplies the preset's overall automatic build speed. Range `0.25–4.0`. |
| **Horizontal Motion Sensitivity** | `1.0` | Left/right and forward/back response. Range `0.95–1.10`. |
| **Vertical Motion Sensitivity** | `1.2` | Up/down response. Range `0.90–1.30`; `1.2` is recommended for small-travel bouncing and squats. |
| **Preview Direction** | Mixed | Changes only the graph preview. All directions remain active in game. |
| **Movement Response** | Folded | Shows the current threshold, saturation, smoothing, and directional response. It is a preview only. |
| **Accumulation Profile** | Uniform | Shapes build speed across the current Sweat % without adding a synced parameter or runtime layer. |
| **Detailed Values** | Folded | For an enabled curve, edits the five multipliers at `0/25/50/75/100%`. Range `0.1–2.0`. |
| **Accumulation Graphs** | Folded | Shows the profile and an estimated timeline to 95% under steady movement. Actual PhysBone input depends on the avatar and animation. |

Accumulation profiles:

- **Uniform:** the same multiplier at every Sweat %; new installations start here.
- **Natural (Recommended):** slightly faster early build and gradually slower high-Sweat build.
- **Fast Start:** strongly emphasizes the beginning.
- **Late Acceleration:** starts slowly and becomes faster later.
- **Custom:** enables direct editing of the five points.

The supplied profiles keep the estimated time to 95% nearly equal; the visible progression and final 5% can differ. Sensitivity, build-speed multiplier, directional sensitivity, and the accumulation profile all affect Auto Mode together.

### Surface and general controls

| Control | Module | Default | Meaning / load |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Sweat Quality** | All | Low Load (Recommended) | Same three tiers as Setup. Material quality updates immediately; generated FX require **Apply to FX**. |
| **Sweat Style** (**Basic Settings**) | Base | Anime | Changes the style of every sweat material of the avatar at once. |
| **Droplet Size** | Base | `1.6` | Size of droplets attached to the body. The base size follows the avatar's body size automatically; use this to change it further. Range `0.5–2.0`. |
| **On from the Start** | Base + Droplet Depth | Off | Keeps Droplet Depth on from the start. Turning it on shows a one-time warning about continuous GPU load. |
| **3D Detail Precision** | Base + Droplet Depth | Simplified | Sets how close up the droplets rise (see [Droplet Depth choices](#droplet-depth-choices)). |
| **Follow Body Shadow Strength** | Base | On | Copies rather than continuously links the Body value. Press **Apply to FX** after changing the Body material. |
| **Body Surface Spawn Mask** | Base | — | Limits where attached droplets, wet film, and Tess displacement are generated. Use it for places that should stay dry. |
| **Mesh and Rest Data Diagnostics** | Base | — | Tells you when a material has no valid Rest data, or the mesh changed after the bake. |
| **Retention Time Multiplier** | All | `1.0` | Multiplies dry-off duration. Higher values keep sweat visible longer. Range `0.5–2.0`. |

The Pattern Density control is hidden in 2.0.0.

### Effect Toggles

This section appears only with the **Effects Module**.

| Control | Default | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Falling Drops** | On | Enables falling body droplets. |
| **Bloom Sparkle** | On | Adds a restrained highlight to falling drops. It spreads only in bloom-enabled worlds. |
| **Enable Floor Stains** | On | Enables floor stains from falling drops. Falling Drops must be active to create them. |
| **Breath** | On | Enables the breath emitter. |
| **Body Steam** | On | Enables body steam. |
| **Steam Locations** | Chest, shoulders, armpits, thighs on | Enables the installed emitters. Crotch and feet are off by default. Enabling a location excluded during installation requires **[Install / Update]**. |
| **Body Steam in Mirrors** | Low on new installs | `Full / Low / Hidden`. Updates preserve the old appearance as Full. |
| **Show My Breath in First Person** | Off | Keeps breath visible to yourself. Requires a valid Head bone and a Modular Avatar version that includes MA Visible Head Accessory. |

Custom Steam Points are added in Tuning. Up to four custom emitter slots can be active, a mirrored pair uses two, and twelve combined built-in/custom emitters is the hard limit. A combined rate weight over `4.0` is normalized proportionally.

## Advanced FX Tuning

Advanced controls modify generated animation or particle assets and require **Apply to FX**. Use **Restore Category Defaults** to reset only one group, or **Reset Advanced FX Values** for all advanced groups.

### Automatic Response

| Control | Default | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Response Threshold** | Balanced `0.04`; Sensitive `0.015` | Motion level where automatic build starts. |
| **Saturation Point** | Balanced `0.35`; Sensitive `0.18` | Motion level where the rise rate reaches full response. |
| **Smoothing** | Balanced `0.12`; Sensitive `0.16` | Higher values respond faster; lower values feel heavier. |
| **Sweat Accumulation Resistance (%)** | `100%` | Gradually resists high-Sweat buildup while preserving full-strength motion. `0%` restores the legacy response. |
| **Movement Resume Delay** | `1 s` | Delay after locomotion or jumping stops before pendulum motion can raise sweat again. |
| **Drop Recovery Time** | `3 s` | How quickly falling-drop activity settles after movement stops. |
| **Exertion Recovery Time** | `9 s` | Recovery of the intermediate exertion stage used by body heat, not breath visibility. |
| **Body Heat Recovery Time** | `30 s` | Recovery of the body-steam cadence modifier. |
| **Pendulum Bone** | Hips | Motion sensor attachment. Chest, head, and hands are available; head/hands can overreact, especially with Sensitive. |

### Effects Module advanced groups

| Group | User-facing controls | Category defaults |
|---|---|---|
| **Falling Drops** | Rate, fall speed, lifetime, size, and length multipliers; Strong Movement Fling; fling threshold, chance, cooldown, and burst min/max | Multipliers `1.0`; fling on; threshold/chance `0.55`; cooldown `3.5 s`; burst `2–5` |
| **Floor Stains** | Lifetime, size, opacity, center-drying contrast, pooling chance | Multipliers `1.0`; contrast `0.75`; pooling `0` |
| **Body Steam** | Start threshold; density, rise speed, lifetime, size, spread, opacity | Threshold `30%`; multipliers `1.0` |
| **Breath** | Start threshold; mouth gate; mouth parameter and threshold; amount, speed, lifetime, size, spread, opacity, cycle speed | Threshold `25%`; gate Auto; parameter `v2/JawOpen`; mouth threshold `0.2`; multipliers `1.0` |
| **Performance Limits** | Drop, stain, steam, and breath maximum-particle limits | **Drop Particle Limit (Per System)** `12` (strong-movement drops use two thirds of it, 8), stain `80`, steam `24` per emitter, breath `48` |

Steam and 3D breath use only as many particles as they need, never more than the limit; 2D breath uses the value as is. The drop limit default changed from 64 to 12 in v2.0.0 because the drops fall faster, so at most 8 drops of a system are in the air at once.

**Breath Mouth Gate** options:

- **Always:** breath is not gated by mouth movement.
- **Viseme (lipsync):** uses VRChat viseme lip sync.
- **Custom Parameter (VRCFT):** uses the selected float parameter and mouth-open threshold.
- **Auto (active VRCFT, Viseme fallback):** starts on Viseme and prefers VRCFT after detecting a real `JawOpen` open/close change. Once JawOpen has gone down to a closed value (below 0.015) and 8 seconds pass without it rising above 0.03, it returns to Viseme.

## Sweat material inspector

These are material controls, not fields on the Tuning component. Open the generated RealSweat material Inspector. It shows only what the new look reads: **Quick Setup**, **Distribution**, **Flow**, **Lighting**, and **Advanced**. The 1.1.x values are hidden but kept in the material.

| Control | Location | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest Data** | Top of the inspector | Tells you when the material has no valid Rest data. |
| **Glint Size** / **Glint Strength** | **Quick Setup** | The light's glint on the droplets. Both are `1.5` by default; `1` gives a calmer look. |
| **Sweat Flow** | **Quick Setup** | How many droplets run down steep skin, and how far. The default `1` is the standard look of each style; `0` keeps the droplets in place, `2` lets most of them run. |
| **Pattern Seed** / **New Pattern** | **Distribution** | The avatar's pattern number. **New Pattern** changes it. |
| **Reset Look** | Inspector button | Resets only the look values and keeps the amount, quality, pattern number, masks, stencil, Rest data, and the Setup choices. The presets of 1.1.x were replaced by this one button. |

### Surface masks

- **Body Surface Spawn Mask** limits where attached droplets, wet film, and Tess displacement are generated. Use the Tuning's **Body Surface Spawn Mask** for places that should stay dry.
- **Body Surface Display Mask** supports R/G/B/A channel selection, inversion, strength, tiling, and offset. It hides the complete final surface result, including droplets, trails, film, sheen, refraction, darkening, and Tess displacement, but not Falling Drops particles. For accurate grayscale values, import the Display Mask with **sRGB (Color Texture) Off**. Its default strength is `0`, so updating does not change the existing appearance until you configure it.
- Both masks use UV0.

### Removed in v2.0.0

Milk mode, the body normal map's wet effect, the optical LUT, the flow guide map, and the Rest pattern rotation are not part of the 2.0.0 look. The manual Rest axis modes of Pattern Space (Rest Front XY／Side ZY／Top XZ) are gone too; materials that used them change to Rest Normal Triplanar. The `Body Normal Map` value stays in the material but has no effect, and flow direction follows live world gravity. The per-mesh pattern density and the Tuning's Pattern Density are hidden too.

When Light Limit Changer (LLC) changes the avatar's brightness, the skin sweat follows with the same ratio. See [Compatibility](../compatibility.mdx#light-limit-changer).

## Remote Low Load and mirrors

**Remote Low Load** is an avatar-menu control, not a Tuning Inspector field. Other users' screens draw No Tess without the small pore droplets, and at Low Load and Lightweight also without the residual beads and Anime's thin film lines, with less work where two surface directions meet (about 4-8% less GPU time for them at Low Load, 23-29% at Standard). Remote Body／Custom Steam keeps its puffs at 0.6× density (3D body steam: 25% of the puffs). With 3D Particle Quality, it halves remote Falling Drop emission and disables remote strong-motion fling. Local quality is preserved.

Mirror options:

- **High-Quality Sweat in Mirrors** is off by default. While it is off, mirrors leave out the small pore droplets and Droplet Depth does not rise there. When it is on, mirrors draw the same as the normal view.
- **Body Steam in Mirrors** offers Full, Low, and Hidden. 2D `Low` draws the steam in mirrors at 0.8× the density of `Full` without reducing particle count. 3D `Low` currently uses the same appearance and cost as `Full`. `Hidden` has the lowest mirror cost.

:::warning[Combined GPU load]
Standard quality, close-up screen coverage, mirrors, and Droplet Depth can compound GPU load. Change one major setting at a time and test the uploaded avatar.
:::
