# Purchase Verification

Each PC is activated once with your order number. The activation window is at `CookiTools → RealSweatSystem → Purchase Activation`.

One order covers up to 3 PCs (the Game & App license: 10).

Internet is needed to activate, and at least once every 30 days for the automatic renewal. Building and uploading avatars never needs it.

How to activate, how to release a PC, gifts, and error codes are explained on the page linked below.

- **Details:** [Purchase activation guide](https://cookistudio.com/license/)
- **Privacy:** [Privacy notice for purchase activation](https://cookistudio.com/license/privacy)
